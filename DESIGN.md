## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the warmth, joy, and peace of mind that comes with caring for a cherished pet. It balances the playful enthusiasm of pets with the scientific credibility required for pet nutrition and wellness. The aesthetic bridges contemporary e-commerce utility with warm, tactile hospitality—avoiding clinical sterility while steering clear of overly cartoonish tropes.

The design movement merges **Modern Organic UI** with **Soft Tactile Warmth**:
- **Surfaces & Tones:** Deeply creamy, oatmeal, and honey-milk backgrounds replace harsh digital white, imparting organic calm and domestic comfort.
- **Micro-Delights:** Cheerful amber-tangerine calls-to-action evoke vitality, complemented by nourishing botanical teal-greens that communicate health, organic ingredients, and vet-approved safety.
- **Bilingual Balance:** Engineered for seamless Thai and Latin bilingual typography, offering open counters, generous vertical x-height proportions, and comfortable line heights to accommodate Thai tone marks without line collisions.
- **Emotional Signature:** Warm, welcoming, trustworthy, and organized. The user feels like stepping into a well-curated, sunshine-filled neighborhood pet apothecary.

## Colors

The color palette centers on warm energy balanced by natural botanical reassurance:

- **Primary (`#F26E22` — Amber Tangerine):** Used for primary conversion triggers, cart alerts, focal promotions, and prominent interactive states. It signals warmth, enthusiasm, and appetizing vitality.
- **Secondary (`#1E7B6C` — Forest Teal):** Used for trust badges, nutritional claims, veterinary endorsements, and calm interactive elements. It anchors the energetic orange with organic credibility.
- **Tertiary (`#F4B236` — Honey Gold):** Used for loyalty perks, rating stars, gentle accents, treat badges, and pet life-stage markers.
- **Neutral Core (`#2B2623` — Roasted Espresso):** Replaces pure digital black with a rich, warm dark charcoal-brown. It softens reading contrast, preventing eye fatigue while maintaining WCAG AAA legibility.
- **Background & Canvas:**
  - Base canvas: `#FDFBF7` (Alabaster Cream)
  - Surface containers / Card base: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Cloud)
  - Nested container / Secondary surface: `#F8F3EA` (Warm Oat)
  - Highlight surface / Tinted card: `#FFF6ED` (Apricot Glow)
  - Border sub-tone: `#EFE8DC` (Soft Wheat Line)

## Typography

Typography prioritizes approachable geometric clarity and multi-script legibility.

- **Type Scale Execution:** `plusJakartaSans` provides wide apertures, tall lowercase x-heights, and rounded humanist strokes that harmonize seamlessly with modern Thai sans-serifs (such as Prompt, Kanit, or Sukhumvit).
- **Line Height Compensation:** Line height tokens preserve minimum 1.5x to 1.65x multiplier for body copy to prevent Thai diacritics and upper/lower vowel tone marks (e.g., ไม้เอก, ไม้โท, สระอี, สระอุ) from overlapping adjacent sentences or button bounds.
- **Display Weights:** Heavy weights (700, 800) are reserved for product naming, promotions, and emotional hooks. Body and nutritional ingredient tables maintain clean standard 400 and 600 weights.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptable 12-column grid system resting inside a bounded maximum container width of `1280px`, anchored by generous, breathable margins to keep the shopping journey uncluttered:

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` (16px) margins, `1rem` gutters. Product cards display in 2-column or single horizontal card configurations.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` (32px) margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Product grids display in 3 columns.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout, `3.5rem` (56px) outer margins, `1.75rem` (28px) gutters. Product grids comfortably flow in 4 columns.
- **Rhythm Philosophy:** The system uses an 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-grid for badge padding and nutritional icon layouts. Dense multi-column filters use `0.75rem` vertical spacing, while hero banners and story-driven pet care sections leverage `3rem` to `4rem` spatial breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold, grey industrial drop shadows. Instead, it utilizes **warm amber-sepia ambient diffusions** that mimic daylight landing on cozy surfaces:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Surfaces sit directly on `#FDFBF7` without shadow. Used for page background layers and standard inline text zones.
- **Level 1 (Resting Card):** `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(84, 52, 28, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(84, 52, 28, 0.03);` bordered by a faint `1px solid #EFE8DC`. Used for inactive product cards, review tiles, and nutritional breakdown segments.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Floating):** `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(84, 52, 28, 0.09), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(84, 52, 28, 0.04);` with a slight upward translation (`transform: translateY(-3px)`). Used for hovered product cards, active quick-view trays, and floating category pills.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(43, 38, 35, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(43, 38, 35, 0.08);` paired with a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) over a translucent warm wash (`rgba(43, 38, 35, 0.35)`). Used for cart flyouts, pet profile switchers, and dietary filter dialogs.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature to convey pet-friendly softness, friendly touchpoints, and child-safe approachability without losing geometric cleanliness:

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Form input fields, nutritional data tables, mini tags, and small dropdown selectors.
- **Cards & Banners (1rem / 16px):** Product cards, review containers, delivery perk banners, and category browsing cards.
- **Hero & Modal Sheets (1.5rem / 24px):** Promotional banners, pet wellness onboarding boxes, and drawer overlays.
- **Pills & Badges (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Action buttons, price badges, pet category chips (e.g., "สุนัข / Dog", "แมว / Cat"), dietary indicator tags, and notification counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#F26E22` with white text, fully rounded (`9999px`), bold weight (`label-lg`), subtle inner glow, transitioning to `#D95B13` on hover with Level 2 elevation.
- **Secondary Action:** Translucent teal tint (`#E8F4F2`) with `#1E7B6C` text, fully rounded, transitioning to `#D2ECE8` on hover.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Border `1.5px solid #EFE8DC` on cream background with `#2B2623` text; hover shifts to solid `#FFF6ED` with `#F26E22` border.
- **Sizing:** Large (48px height, 24px x-padding), Medium (40px height, 18px x-padding), Small (32px height, 12px x-padding).

### Product Cards
- **Construction:** Background `#FFFFFF`, rounded `1rem` (16px), subtle border `1px solid #EFE8DC`, Level 1 elevation.
- **Image Container:** Soft cream `#F8F3EA` rounded image pocket (`0.75rem`) with `aspect-ratio: 1/1`, object-contain framing.
- **Badges:** Absolute positioned at top-left/right:
  - Nutritional Badge (e.g., "Grain-Free", "High Protein", "โซเดียมต่ำ"): `#E8F4F2` teal background, `#1E7B6C` text, rounded `9999px`.
  - Discount Badge: Solid `#F26E22` text on `#FFF2EA` pill.
- **Content Flow:** Brand name in `label-md` (`#786E65`), product title in `title-md` 2-line clamp, price row with bold primary price (`headline-sm`) and strikethrough original price, completed with an icon-only or text "Add to Cart" button.

### Chips & Filters
- **Category Filter Pills:** Rounded `9999px`, height `36px`, padding `0 14px`. Inactive state: `#F8F3EA` background, `#2B2623` label. Active state: `#1E7B6C` background, `#FFFFFF` text with checkmark or pet icon prefix.
- **Pet Type Switchers:** Generous circular badge buttons with pet silhouettes (Dog, Cat, Small Pet, Bird) with active ring indicator in `#F26E22`.

### Nutritional & Life-Stage Badges
- Compact rectangular pills (`rounded-lg`, 6px) with an icon and bilingual label (e.g., paw, leaf, heart, bone icons).
- Color-coded by health benefit:
  - Green/Teal (`#E8F4F2` / `#1E7B6C`) for Natural, Digestion, Renal, Hypoallergenic.
  - Orange/Amber (`#FFF2EA` / `#F26E22`) for Energy, Muscle Growth, Immunity.
  - Gold/Yellow (`#FEF7E6` / `#B87B09`) for Coat & Skin, Omega-3, Senior Care.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Selects:** Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #EFE8DC`, rounded `0.5rem` (8px), placeholder in `#A3988E`. Focus state introduces `#F26E22` border with a 3px outer halo in `rgba(242, 110, 34, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom `20px` boxes with rounded `6px` (checkbox) or circular (radio). Unchecked: `#EFE8DC` border. Checked: `#1E7B6C` background with bold white check/dot.

### Pet Profile Bar (Specialized Component)
- Horizontal sticky bar or card letting shoppers toggle active pet profiles (e.g., "Milo — Corgi, 2 yrs", "Luna — British Shorthair, 4 yrs"). Displays a cheerful avatar framed in an amber border with quick tags filtering current catalog compatibility.
